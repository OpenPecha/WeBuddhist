Welcome to WeBuddhist!
We are Buddhists. We learn, practice, and connect — daily.
Why — Our Purpose
We're here because we want to help ourselves and others break free from suffering and find lasting happiness — and we believe being Buddhist is the best way to do
that.
Being Buddhist means accepting three truths and living them:

1. As Buddhists, we accept that everything is impermanent.
2. As Buddhists, we accept that nothing will ever fully satisfy us.
3. As Buddhists, we accept that nothing — not even ourselves — exists as it appears to us.
   WeBuddhist exists so that we — and anyone who wishes — never forget to live these three truths.
   How — The Buddha's Method
   Understanding these truths once isn't enough. To really live them, we need to work at them a little each day. So we make sure to learn, practice, and connect, daily.
   Practically, this comes together in one simple commitment: a five-minute daily dharma routine. Day by day, it guides us to do a little less harm, a little more good, and
   to know our own minds a little better. These three are how the Buddha himself summarized his entire teaching.
   What — Content and Technology
   To make all of this effortless. We build WeBuddhist for five-minute daily practice, WeBuddhist Academy for long-form courses, and the Buddhist Creators Network to
   upskill and support Buddhist content creators, influencers, and educators.
   We build along four pillars:
   Learn — we help Buddhists access the Dhamma in their own words;
   Practice — we help Buddhists be transformed by the Buddha's wisdomthrough daily habits;
   Connect — we help Buddhists share that transformation with others through communities;
   Sustain — we raise the funds to keep our tools free forever and the project sustainable.
   The Team
   Behind the apps and content are many dedicated Buddhist content creators and technology developers, spread across India, Nepal, Taiwan, Canada, Bali, and other
   locations, building together since 2025. Six teams carry on our mission:
   Product Engineering makes daily practice a five-minute habit that sticks.
   Content Engineering and Data Services make Buddhist scriptures relatable yet reliable: engaging, modern content grounded in authentic sources and
   multiplied across languages.
   Community & Campaigns supports Buddhist creators and runs campaigns with them.
   Partnerships & Funding serves large Buddhist groups and proves the mix of funding that keeps our apps free forever, while People & Ops keeps all of us
   running.
